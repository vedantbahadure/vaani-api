# VAANI — Demo Script for Judges (≈4 minutes)

Goal: prove **Trust, Simplicity, Performance, Delight** fast. Keep the internet on.

## 0. Setup (before judges arrive)
- Open `/` on a good screen. Let the **cinematic intro** play once (or press Skip).
- Ensure sound is on. Optionally enable **Wake word** and **Auto-play voice** in Settings.

## 1. The opening (30s) — Delight
- Land on `/`. The particle **awakening → Living Orb → “Hello, I’m VAANI.”** plays.
- Scroll a little: the **10-chapter scroll story** (India Awakens → … → Live Dashboard) with the
  breathing orb and parallax. Say: *“VAANI is the calm, trusted face of rural governance.”*
- Click **Enter VAANI**.

## 2. One-touch Demo Mode (60s) — Performance + Multilingual
- Go to **Chat** → click **Play Demo Mode**.
- It auto-asks 4 questions across **English → Hindi → Marathi**, streaming grounded answers with
  **citations** and a **confidence badge**. Point at the badge: *“Every answer is verified from real
  documents — never invented.”*

## 3. Trust close-up (45s) — Trust
- Ask by text: *“What does crop insurance PMFBY cover and what premium do I pay?”*
- Show: streamed answer, **green Verified · Confidence %**, **Source cards** (PMFBY), inline **[1]**.
- Tap the **Translate** button → the same answer in Hindi. Tap **▶ voice** → word-by-word highlight.

## 4. Voice-first (45s) — Simplicity
- Tap the **mic** (or say **“VAANI”** if wake-word is on). Watch the **listening orb + live waveform**.
- Ask: *“How do I get a Kisan Credit Card?”* → transcribed (Whisper) → grounded answer → spoken back.
- Tap the mic again mid-speech to show **barge-in** (VAANI stops and listens).

## 5. Am I Eligible? (30s) — Real-world value
- Home → **Am I Eligible?** → pick **PM-KISAN**, 1.5 ha, General, not taxpayer, not govt employee →
  **Check eligibility** → grounded verdict with the eligibility criteria and citations.

## 6. Depth & robustness (30s) — Engineering
- **Knowledge**: filter domains, run a semantic search (show similarity scores).
- **Documents**: upload a small PDF/TXT → ask a question → grounded answer from *that* document.
- **System**: `/app/status` — subsystems online, the **RAG pipeline**, KB breakdown, and the
  **Hardware Abstraction** matrix. Say: *“Same code runs on a laptop or a Raspberry Pi kiosk.”*
- Toggle **Dark/Light** and **Device Mode**; flip to **offline** (DevTools) to show cached content.

## One-liners
- “Trust-first RAG: grounded, cited, with a confidence score — it refuses to fabricate.”
- “Voice-first and multilingual — Marathi, Hindi, English — for people new to AI.”
- “Runs offline-friendly on a ₹-modest Raspberry Pi kiosk in a panchayat.”
